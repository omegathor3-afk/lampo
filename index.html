LAMPO

Your AI agent for the computer you control.

A local-first AI agent that can read, write, run commands and operate your PC — under a permission system that answers to you, not to the model. Everything is saved on your machine.

Download for Windows Explore Lampo

WHY LAMPO

An agent that asks permission.

The model proposes actions — the owner's permission layer decides. Every capability has a trust level you set, and untrusted content can never promote itself into instructions.

OWNER CONTROLLED

Permission gate

Filesystem, terminal, computer control, network — each has a trust level: SAFE, CONFIRM or FULL. Denied actions stay denied, no matter how they are worded.

LOCAL-FIRST

Your machine, your data

Conversations, projects and settings are stored locally in SQLite. The cloud is an enhancement, never a requirement.

MODEL INDEPENDENT

Bring your own model

Local GGUF via llama.cpp, Ollama, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint. The model is the engine — Lampo is the agent around it.

AND IT FEELS ALIVE

Calm, cinematic, quietly powerful.

A deep-space workspace with a breathing AI core. Lampo shows you what it is doing — thinking, reading, executing — and asks for confirmation the moment an action needs it.
