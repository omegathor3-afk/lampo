PRIVACY

What leaves your machine — and what never does.

Stored locally

Conversations, tool results, projects, settings and history live in data/ on your machine (SQLite + JSON). Nothing is uploaded automatically.

Sent to the model provider

Your messages and tool context go to whichever AI provider you configure — a local model keeps that on your machine; cloud providers see what you send them.

Never uploaded automatically

Arbitrary files, credentials, tokens, unrestricted tool output and system information are never collected. Cloud sync, when it exists, is opt-in per conversation.

Telemetry

None. Lampo has no analytics or telemetry in v1.0.0.

Authentication

Google sign-in uses a browser PKCE flow — passwords are never entered in Lampo, and tokens are stored encrypted with Windows DPAPI.

Deletion

Delete any conversation permanently in History. Account data can be removed by signing out and deleting data/account.json and data/tokens.vault.
