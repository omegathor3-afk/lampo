SUPPORT

Help when something breaks.

Common issues

Model not responding — check the model server health endpoint in Settings.
Tool denied — your owner policy blocked it; adjust trust in Owner control.
Voice silent — check the voice engine and volume in Settings.

Diagnostics

Lampo writes a rolling log to data/logs/omega_lampo.log. Include the last lines when reporting a problem.

Contact

Use the contact page — categories for bugs, security and general questions. The contact backend is configuration-required; no personal addresses are exposed.
